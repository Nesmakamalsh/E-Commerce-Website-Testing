
# Test Scenarios

This document contains the high-level scenarios identified for the SauceDemo application.

## 1. Login

- Verify login with valid credentials.
- Verify login with invalid credentials.
- Verify login with an empty username.
- Verify login with an empty password.
- Verify login behaviour for a locked-out user.

## 2. Products

- Verify that the inventory page is displayed after login.
- Verify product names, descriptions, and prices.
- Verify product sorting by name.
- Verify product sorting by price.
- Verify product details navigation.

## 3. Shopping Cart

- Verify adding a product to the cart.
- Verify adding multiple products to the cart.
- Verify removing a product from the cart.
- Verify cart contents and item count.
- Verify navigation between the inventory page and cart.

## 4. Checkout

- Verify checkout navigation.
- Verify checkout with valid customer information.
- Verify validation when required information is missing.
- Verify checkout overview information.

## 5. Order Completion

- Verify item totals and calculated amounts.
- Verify successful order completion.
- Verify the order confirmation message.
- Verify returning to the products page after completion.

## Notes

These are initial test scenarios. They will be refined and expanded during application exploration and test case design.
